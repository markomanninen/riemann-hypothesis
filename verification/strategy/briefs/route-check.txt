You are an independent checker of a commentary on a Lean 4 proof. The commentary is
verification/strategy/README.md in this repository, and the Lean source is under src/. Read the
files you need, write one report, and change no other file.

The commentary will be the source of a public explainer video, so a wrong claim in it would be
repeated in public. Your task is to test whether the Lean text supports what the commentary says
about the route of the proof. You test the commentary against the Lean text. Whether the
mathematics is true is outside this task. Treat the commentary as a list of claims to test, and
trust only what you see in the Lean files.

Check these parts, in this order of importance.

1. The nine numbered steps under the heading "The route in nine steps".
2. The section "From the two bounds on the slope to the exponent 1/2". Above all the four terms
   that the division of the error bracket leaves, and the claim that the exponent 1/2 comes from
   the term x/m against the term m. Compute the four terms yourself from the definitions
   errorBracketSizeWindows, RestSize and BNBounds and from the hypotheses of ch_core. Check what
   the conditions DIV and SlopeIneq say, with the direction of each inequality.
3. The subsection "The pair of primes". Above all what pair_share, stage, bad_share, meanSq_abs,
   twistK, box_of_S and count_bad state, every exponent and share, and how stage sorts the bad
   primes.
4. The sections "The upper bound for the lattices of a datum", "The chain of lifts", "The bound
   for one lift" and "The lower bound for the lattices of a datum". Above all the claim that each
   map is a composite of a chain of m theta kernels between unitary groups of ranks k + j(t + 1),
   the claim that interlacing weights leave one term a log a - a for each lift and top place, and
   what the three bounds of LinkS_at say.
5. The section "From a bound on intervals to zero-freeness", the Landau step in four stages.
6. In the section "Words used here", the two list items on the slope of a lattice and the height
   of a map.

For each claim that you test, open the Lean file that the commentary links or names, find the
declaration, and compare the statement. Compare the proof as well where the commentary describes
the proof. The source has about 149,000 modules, so search for a name with a text search over
src/ and read only the files you need. The script verification/strategy/cone.py finds a compiled
constant by name, and its header explains the usage.

Write the report as Markdown to verification/review/strategy-route-check/gemini.md, with three
parts.

- Part A, findings. Each finding has the quoted sentence of the commentary, the Lean file and
  line that contradicts it or fails to support it, the Lean lines themselves, and a corrected
  sentence. Order the findings by how visible the claim would be in a public explainer of the
  nine steps. Give at most 25 findings.
- Part B, confirmed claims. One line for each claim that you compared with the Lean text and
  found supported, with the file and line.
- Part C, claims that you could not test, each with the reason.

Mark every statement of your own that goes beyond the Lean text as an interpretation. Write the
prose in full sentences.
