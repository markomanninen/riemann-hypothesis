You are an independent checker of two documents about a Lean 4 proof. The documents are
verification/strategy/CLAIMS.md and verification/strategy/README.md in this repository, and the
Lean source is under src/. Read the files you need, write one report, and change no other file.

CLAIMS.md lists the claims that carry the route of the proof. README.md is the commentary behind
it. The claims will be the source of public explainer videos, so a wrong claim would be repeated
in public. Your task is to test whether the Lean text supports each claim. You test the documents
against the Lean text. Whether the mathematics is true is outside this task. Treat every claim as
something to test, and trust only what you see in the Lean files.

First part, the claims. Take every table row of CLAIMS.md in order.

1. Open each declaration that the row names in its column of declarations. The source has about
   149,000 modules, so search for a name with a text search over src/ and read only the files you
   need. The script verification/strategy/cone.py finds a compiled constant by name, and its
   header explains the usage.
2. Compare the row with the statement of the declaration, and with its proof where the row
   speaks about the proof. A row has to say what the Lean text states, neither more nor less.
3. Record a verdict for the row. The verdicts are supported, says more, says less, differs, and
   could not test. For every verdict other than supported, quote the Lean lines with file and
   line, say what they state, and write a corrected row.

Then take the lists under the tables. An entry there is an explanation, an interpretation or a
finding. Report an entry that the Lean text contradicts.

Second part, the commentary. Check these parts of README.md in the same way, as far as they go
beyond the claims.

1. The section "From the two bounds on the slope to the exponent 1/2". Compute the four terms
   that the division of the error bracket leaves yourself, from the definitions
   errorBracketSizeWindows, RestSize and BNBounds and from the hypotheses of ch_core.
2. The subsection "The pair of primes".
3. The sections "The upper bound for the lattices of a datum", "The chain of lifts", "The bound
   for one lift" and "The lower bound for the lattices of a datum".
4. The section "From a bound on intervals to zero-freeness", the Landau step in four stages.

Write the report as Markdown to verification/review/strategy-route-check/gemini.md, with four
parts.

- Part A, a table with one line for each row of CLAIMS.md, with the number of the row, the
  verdict and the file and line of the Lean text that you compared it with.
- Part B, the findings on the claims, one for each verdict other than supported, ordered by the
  number of the row.
- Part C, the findings on the commentary, at most 20, each with the quoted sentence, the Lean
  file and line, the Lean lines themselves and a corrected sentence.
- Part D, what you could not test, each with the reason.

Mark every statement of your own that goes beyond the Lean text as an interpretation. Write the
prose in full sentences.
