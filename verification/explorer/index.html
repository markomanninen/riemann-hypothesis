How to read this page

The submission proves the Riemann Hypothesis from allChi, which says that no Dirichlet L-function vanishes for Re s > 1/2. The proof of allChi is the theorem everyCharacter in the module Main Conclusion, and 175,174 project modules lie below it.

In the landscape, a module is a dot. Its size is its line count. The rings on the ground are the source folders, which the author named after areas of mathematics, and a folder's ring encloses its modules and subfolders. A module's height is the number of modules in the longest chain of imports below it, so a module that imports only Mathlib lies on the ground and Main Conclusion is the summit. The Longest chain button draws one such chain from the summit to the ground.

Selecting a module colours two sets. Orange marks every module it imports, directly or through other modules. Green marks every module that imports it in the same way. Selecting a ring shows that folder's modules alone.

Under a selected module the page lists the module's statements. A statement is a declaration as the Lean source writes it, with the proof left out. For a theorem, the part marked given holds the objects and assumptions, and the part marked claim holds what the theorem says about them. A definition also shows what it is defined as. File variables are declared once in the file, and Lean adds to a statement those of them that the statement uses.

The branches view is the dominator tree of the import graph. A module B sits under a module A when every chain of imports from the summit down to B passes through A. A branch therefore holds the modules that only its top module needs.

Everything here is built from import lines and a text search of the sources. The search splits a statement at the first colon and the first := outside brackets, so a rare layout can place the split wrongly, and the source link shows the original. A module shown here may still go unused by the proof terms. Which declarations the proof really uses will follow from the declaration graph of the Comparator export.
