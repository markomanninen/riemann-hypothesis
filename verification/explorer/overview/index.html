A Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, and what has been checked of it

Levent Alpöge announced on 20 November 2026 a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis written in Lean 4, a language in which a program checks every step of a proof. This page leads to the work that reads the proof. It says what the proof claims, what has been checked and by whom, where each part can be read, and what is open.

Open the proof explorer

What the proof claims

The file Main.lean proves five theorems. The first is the Riemann Hypothesis, and the other four state it for the L-functions of Dirichlet characters.

  • riemannHypothesisMathlib's statement of the Riemann Hypothesis.
  • riemannZeta_eq_zero_reThe same statement, written out.
  • LFunction_ne_zero_of_half_lt_reL(χ, s) ≠ 0 for Re s > 1/2, for every modulus and every Dirichlet character, except the trivial character at s = 1.
  • LFunction_eq_zero_reEvery zero of L(χ, s) with 0 < Re s < 1 has Re s = 1/2.
  • LFunction_eq_zero_re_of_isPrimitiveThe same, for primitive characters.

What has been checked, and by whom

Axioms

A script read the compiled project constant by constant and followed every constant that the five theorems use, 1,491,293 in all. The only axioms among them are Classical.choice, Quot.sound and propext, the three that Lean's own library uses.

Kernel replay

Comparator is the tool that replays a proof in Lean's kernel and in a second, independent checker. The notes of the people who check the submission do not yet record a result of its run.

Readings

A language model, Claude, wrote what each of the 40 largest steps states, and further runs of the same model checked every reading against the Lean text. A model of another maker, Gemini, then read the readings again. Its findings changed 40 passages in 16 readings and 103 passages in the 16 deeper readings. None of the findings that held was a sentence that the Lean text contradicts.

After the corrections

A further check passed 14 of the 16 corrected deeper readings and found 2 errors in the others, which were corrected. A check of the corrected texts then found no error.

Mathematics

No mathematician has checked the readings or the account of the strategy. Both say what the Lean text states. Whether a statement labelled as published agrees with the literature is a separate question, and nobody has compared them.

The route of the proof in nine steps

The account of the strategy follows the proof from the five theorems down to two bounds on one lattice. It numbers the steps of that route and gives each a short name.

Read the nine steps on GitHub

  1. Reduction of the five theorems to zero-freeness above 1/2
  2. Landau argument from a bound x^θ on the prime sum Gm
  3. Host field that holds the kept characters, and a bound for the other characters
  4. Fourier inversion from the sum over characters to ASK
  5. Upper and lower bound on the slope of one lattice
  6. Chain of m kernels for the pairing of each word
  7. Upper bound for one lift from interlacing weights and a hook product
  8. Lower bound from many maps into one lattice
  9. Window for m that keeps the error terms x/m and m near √x

What is open

  • Gemini has read the first versions of the readings and has not read the corrected texts.
  • No mathematician has compared a statement labelled as published with its source in the literature.
  • A model of another maker, Gemini, is to test the claims of the strategy against the Lean text. The account of the strategy has so far been written and checked by one model. Noted on 2026-11-24.
  • A pull request to the repository of the checking group could remove the definitions that are always true. It has not been written. Noted on 2026-11-24.
  • A document for a number theorist, with every statement that the proof takes from the literature next to its source, has not been written. Noted on 2026-11-24.
