s0-route · r. 3 Väite T

The proof derives the five theorems from one inequality on a weighted prime sum, which it gets by bounding the slope of one lattice from two sides with m near √x

The proof in src derives the five theorems of Main.lean from one inequality. The first theorem states that a Dirichlet L-function has no zero with real part above 1/2, and the other four follow from it. The inequality says that a weighted sum over the prime powers up to x, with its main terms subtracted, is at most x^(1/2 + cε) for all x between Y and Y^(3/2). This holds for every small ε > 0 and for arbitrarily large Y, with a constant c that depends on neither ε nor Y. The proof gets the inequality by bounding the slope of one lattice from two sides. A divisibility inequality bounds that slope, together with a height term, from above by m times a main term, where m is the number of theta lifts in a chain between unitary groups. A slope inequality bounds the same slope from below. The two bounds leave error terms of about x/m and about m for the weighted sum, and the proof assumes that m lies in a window around √x, where both terms are about √x.
